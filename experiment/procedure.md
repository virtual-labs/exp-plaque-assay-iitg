### Procedure

#### Cell culture (Day 0)
1. **Cell maintenance:** Maintain the BHK-21 cells in T-25 flasks containing cell maintenance medium in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂. Visualize the cells until they reach sufficient confluency for seeding.
2. **Seeding:** Trypsinize a confluent T-25 flask of BHK cells. Count the cells and make a 12 mL suspension of $1 \times 10^5$ to $1.5 \times 10^5$ cells/mL density in DMEM media. Seed a 12-well plate by adding 1 mL of the suspension to each well. Incubate the cells at 37°C in a 5% CO₂ incubator overnight to achieve 100% confluency the next day.

#### Virus dilution, infection of cells (Day 1)
1. Label 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes as $10^{-1}$, $10^{-2}$, $10^{-3}$, $10^{-4}$, $10^{-5}$, and $10^{-6}$. Add 450 μL of plain DMEM to each tube, and 50 μL of viral supernatant to the tube labelled $10^{-1}$. Vortex and thoroughly mix the dilution.
2. Serially dilute the virus 10-fold by transferring 50 μL viral supernatant to $10^{-2}$ vortexing and transferring 100 μL to the subsequent tube, down to $10^{-6}$.
3. Label the seeded 12-well plate according to the figure, remove the media from the well and give a gentle wash with 1x PBS. Add 100 μL of plain DMEM media in the control well, neat viral supernatant in the neat labelled well, and $10^{-1}$, $10^{-2}$, $10^{-3}$, $10^{-4}$, $10^{-5}$, and $10^{-6}$ dilutions in designated wells by changing tips every time. Incubate at 37°C in a 5% CO₂ incubator for 1 hour (Infection time may vary with virus), rocking every 15 min.
4. After incubation, discard the virus and gently wash with 1x PBS to remove loosely adsorbed or non-adsorbed viral particles. Add fresh methylcellulose DMEM media (1 mL/well) and incubate the plate for at least 72 hours.

#### Fixation, staining, plaque counting, and titre calculation (Day 4)
1. After 72 hours of incubation, discard the methylcellulose media. Add 500 μL/well fixing agent (Methanol) to each well and keep it on slow shaking for 10-15 min.
2. Remove the methanol and stain the cells with 1% crystal violet; keep the plate on slow shaking for 10-15 min. Remove the 1% crystal violet and wash the cells using water.
3. Observe and count the plaques.

#### Titre Calculation
$$\text{Infectivity titer (PFU/mL)} = \text{Plaque number} \times \frac{1}{\text{Dilution}} \times \frac{1}{\text{Volume used}}$$

For example, if you count 50 plaques from a plate inoculated with 0.1 mL of a $10^{-6}$ dilution:
$$\text{Infectivity titer} = 50 \times \frac{1}{10^{-6}} \times \frac{1}{0.1\text{ mL}}$$

$$\text{Infectivity titer} = 50 \times 10^6 \times 10 = 5 \times 10^8\text{ PFU/mL}$$
