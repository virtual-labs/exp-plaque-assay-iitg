Plaque assay for the quantification of the virus

Procedure

Cell culture (Day 0)

  1. Cell maintenance: Maintain the BHK-21 cells in T-25 flasks containing cell maintenance medium in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂. Visualize the cells until they reach sufficient confluency for seeding.
  2. Seeding: Trypsinize a confluent T-25 flask of BHK cells. Count the cells and make a 12 mL suspension of 1×1051 \times 10^51×105 to 1.5×1051.5 \times 10^51.5×105 cells/mL density in DMEM media. Seed a 12-well plate by adding 1 mL of the suspension to each well. Incubate the cells at 37°C in a 5% CO₂ incubator overnight to achieve 100% confluency the next day.

Virus dilution, infection of cells (Day 1)

  1. Label 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes as 10−110^{-1}10−1, 10−210^{-2}10−2, 10−310^{-3}10−3, 10−410^{-4}10−4, 10−510^{-5}10−5, and 10−610^{-6}10−6. Add 450 μL of plain DMEM to each tube, and 50 μL of viral supernatant to the tube labelled 10−110^{-1}10−1. Vortex and thoroughly mix the dilution.
  2. Serially dilute the virus 10-fold by transferring 50 μL viral supernatant to 10−210^{-2}10−2 vortexing and transferring 100 μL to the subsequent tube, down to 10−610^{-6}10−6.
  3. Label the seeded 12-well plate according to the figure, remove the media from the well and give a gentle wash with 1x PBS. Add 100 μL of plain DMEM media in the control well, neat viral supernatant in the neat labelled well, and 10−110^{-1}10−1, 10−210^{-2}10−2, 10−310^{-3}10−3, 10−410^{-4}10−4, 10−510^{-5}10−5, and 10−610^{-6}10−6 dilutions in designated wells by changing tips every time. Incubate at 37°C in a 5% CO₂ incubator for 1 hour (Infection time may vary with virus), rocking every 15 min.
  4. After incubation, discard the virus and gently wash with 1x PBS to remove loosely adsorbed or non-adsorbed viral particles. Add fresh methylcellulose DMEM media (1 mL/well) and incubate the plate for at least 72 hours.

Fixation, staining, plaque counting, and titre calculation (Day 4)

  1. After 72 hours of incubation, discard the methylcellulose media. Add 500 μL/well fixing agent (Methanol) to each well and keep it on slow shaking for 10-15 min.
  2. Remove the methanol and stain the cells with 1% crystal violet; keep the plate on slow shaking for 10-15 min. Remove the 1% crystal violet and wash the cells using water.
  3. Observe and count the plaques.
